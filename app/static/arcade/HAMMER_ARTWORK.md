# Hammer-Time sprite source

The `hammer-characters.png` transparent atlas was generated with the built-in image generation tool using the five user-supplied reference photographs. Top-left is the player, top-right the braided opponent, bottom-left the cream-jacket opponent, and bottom-right the red-dress opponent. The mallet and hit effects are drawn separately in `hammer_art.js`.

Prompt used:

Use case: style-transfer. Asset type: a single transparent PNG sprite atlas for the side scrolling arcade game Hammer-Time. Reference images 1 and 2 show the same player character, use his black curly hair, beard, recognisable profile, black 1970s suit, bow tie, white shirt and flared trousers. References 3,4,5 are the three other adult character designs. Produce ONE square atlas with EXACTLY FOUR equally sized cells in a 2 by 2 grid, no visible grid or text. Each cell has a single fully visible standing full body sprite centred horizontally with feet at the same baseline, generous transparent margins and no overlap into another cell. Top-left: player facing right in side/three-quarter view, arms bent in front ready to hold a weapon, but DO NOT include a weapon (mallet rendered in code). Top-right: woman from reference 3, preserve blonde braids, pink top, silver skirt, one leg, and both crutches, facing left. Bottom-left: woman from reference 4, preserve long dark hair, cream jacket, orange skirt, patterned tights, black boots and handbag, facing left. Bottom-right: woman from reference 5, preserve dark hair, black jacket, red dress, black stockings, heels and handbag, facing left. Consistent expressive hand-painted 2D arcade sprite style with crisp bold outlines and readable facial detail. Adults fully clothed. Neutral standing/walking-ready poses only; no violence, injuries or text. No scenery, no floor, no shadows outside characters. Genuinely transparent background.
